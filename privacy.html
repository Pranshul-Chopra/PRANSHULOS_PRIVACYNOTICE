PranshulOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

PranshulOS is a fully local desktop application. It does not connect to the internet, collect any personal data, or transmit anything to any server.

Data We Collect

We collect no data. PranshulOS stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • ticket data,routine data,anayltics data, chibi data, configurable launcher data , weekly routine data,document data
  • Application preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device.

Internet Access

PranshulOS does not make any internet connections. It works fully offline.

Third-Party Services

PranshulOS does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, analytics, advertising, or tracking systems.

No Account Required

PranshulOS does not require you to create an account. We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.

Children's Privacy

PranshulOS is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
